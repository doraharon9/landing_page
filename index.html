The Problem

Founders know they need great product videos: walkthroughs, teasers, onboarding clips, feature explainers, social snippets — the works.

But building them is a time sink. You have to script, screen-record, re-record, edit, narrate, and keep everything updated anytime your product changes.

The result? Most startups either skip video altogether or settle for outdated, low-quality versions that don't reflect the product's true value.

The ExplainIt Solution

ExplainIt learns your product — literally by using it — and transforms that understanding into polished videos.

No scripting. No editing. No filming. No added workload.
